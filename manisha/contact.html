FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to common MANISHA FITNESS HUB enquiries.

Do I need previous gym experience?
No. MANISHA FITNESS HUB offers beginner-friendly programs. New members can learn basic exercises and proper gym techniques before progressing to more challenging workouts.
Can I get personal training?
Yes. Personal training sessions are available by appointment. A trainer can guide you through exercises and help structure your training routine according to your goals.
What programs are available?
MANISHA FITNESS HUB provides programs such as strength training, cardio and endurance, functional fitness, beginner fitness, HIIT and personal training.
How can I enquire about membership?
You can contact MANISHA FITNESS HUB by phone, email or through the enquiry form on this page. Select "Membership" in the subject field and send your requirements.
Can I visit the gym before joining?
Yes. Contact the MANISHA FITNESS HUB team to ask about visiting the facility, available timings and membership information before making your decision.
